The LaunchPoint Purpose

LaunchPoint Fitness Training is personal training that teaches clients to eventually work out independently. I started it after watching dozens of friends and acquaintances avoid the gym because they didn’t feel equipped to navigate strength training.

Each six-week block is built on progressive overload, gradually increasing the demands on your body to build muscle and fitness. Every session is also a structured lesson in how to train safely and effectively without a personal trainer, while we get you strong and fit.

~ Erica

About

Erica brings 25 years of teaching and professional coaching to her personal training. With a B.S. in Education, her career spans Division I collegiate rowing, CrossFit coaching and elite-level competition, leadership in wellness for older adults, and personal training for adolescents with ASD and dementia patients. Erica meets each client where they are and implements clear, manageable plans.

Her coaching experience is broad and uncommon: she has taught children foundational movement, trained young adults on the autism spectrum, coached collegiate and competitive athletes, introduced adults to Olympic lifting, and led centenarians through chair yoga, aquatics, and group fitness.

As a youth athlete she held swim team pool records at the community pool and played high school varsity tennis, soccer, and softball. She rowed for the University of Dayton in the nationally ranked lightweight 8+, then moved into competitive CrossFit, finishing near the top 1% in the CrossFit Open multiple times between 2013 and 2017. Today she trains at Pisgah Fitness and is an avid 4.0 tennis player working toward 4.5.

A mother of two, sister, partner, and the family cats’ best friend, Erica balances elite-level knowledge with real-life empathy and accessibility, making high-quality coaching attainable and sustainable. She looks forward to working with you.

LaunchPoint Philosophy

“If exercise could be packed in a pill, it would be the single most widely prescribed and beneficial medicine in the nation.”~ Dr. Robert Butler

The ability to move freely and stay strong throughout life is one of the greatest predictors of longevity and quality of life. My program combines evidence-based strength training, targeted mobility, and mindful recovery to help you build strength and power that lasts: fitness that supports how you want to live, not just how you train.

The goal is for every client to train confidently and independently after our training block. The six-week blocks are structured to teach proper form and how to manage your way around the gym. You’ll develop the skills to manage your own progress, understand recovery, and adjust your training as life changes. This model values education over dependency.

We do NOT want to keep you in an endless loop of sessions. The goal is to empower you to move well, lift safely, and live actively for years to come.
